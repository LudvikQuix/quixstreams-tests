

Description of task


Inputs:
- requested power
- ambient temperature
- chiller setting (states 0 - not running, 1 - low cooling power 2 - high cooling power)
- heater setting (states 0 - not running, 1 - low heating power 2 - high heating power)

Internal states:
- SOC(SOE)
- open circuit terminal voltages
- voltage of RC1 and RC2
- DC voltages on terminal output
- temperature of battery
- DC voltages on terminal output
- DC current

Outputs:
- all internal states

Parameterization:
- max charge capacity
- coeficient for thermal function
- open circuit voltage look-up-table
- parametrization of RC1 and RC2 defined as Tau1 and Tau2 in transfer function
- chiller power (0 -> 0W, 1 -> 2500W, 2 -> 5000W)
- heater power (0 -> 0W, 1 -> 2500W, 2 -> 5000W)
- sampletime
- coolant temperature
- max battery temperature saturation
 


Internal states:
- SOC(SOE)
- open circuit terminal voltages
- voltage of RC1 and RC2
- DC voltages on terminal output
- temperature of battery
- DC voltages on terminal output
- derating temperature look-up-table





Principles:
use this schematic model of battery https://www.google.com/url?sa=t&source=web&rct=j&url=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.researchgate.net%2Ffigure%2FSecond-order-RC-equivalent-circuit-model-consisting-of-two-RC-circuits-and-a-resistor-in_fig1_370697629&ved=0CBYQjRxqFwoTCKDehNv-mZMDFQAAAAAdAAAAABAH&opi=89978449

run discrete simulation with 100ms sample time

thermal function si defined as HeatDiff = kt2*DCCurrent^2 + kt1*DCCurrent + kt0 - ke * (TempOfbattery - TAmbient) + PowerOfHeater - PowerOfChiller
Heat = HeatPrevious + Heatdiff
T = A * Heat 

SOC is calculated as:
Qact = Qprev + DCcurrent * sampletime
Qact should be saturated to 0 and Qmax(maximum charge)
at Qact = 0 battery cannot provide any more DC current(negative values)
at Qact = QMax battery cannot receive  any more DC current(positive values)

OCV of battery is calcualted from look-up-table by inputing QAct

terminalk voltage of battery must contain all 4 elemnts
Static ressitance voltage, RC1 and RC2 voltage and OCV of battery
send all values in battery-data topic


Dc current is calcualted as DC current = RequetedPower / (DCVoltage on battery terminals). 
Negative power request is expected for discharging the battery and positive for charging
